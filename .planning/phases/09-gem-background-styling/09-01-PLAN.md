from Phase 8) through to actual `SetBackgroundColor` emission. Priced gem blocks must render the

`SetBackgroundColor`). Verisium and all other currency categories keep the plain Dracula
background (no override). Re-baseline the seed fixture + insta snapshot in this phase so
`cargo test` ends green.

(the two specific gem colors), making priced gem drops read as a distinct economy category.
