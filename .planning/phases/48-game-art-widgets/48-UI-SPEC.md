# Phase 48 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for Phase 48 (Game-Art Widgets). Generated by gsd-ui-researcher
> in an autonomous run (the author was not available); verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> This is a Rust / egui overlay drawn with the PoE2 game skin, not a web UI: "px" in the template
> sense means **egui points (pt)**, art pixel sizes are stated as "art px" (the 4K game files).

| Property | Value |

|-------|-------------------------------------|--------|-------------------|
| Toggle plate, expanded (chevron up over dot) | `common/toggle_collapse_{normal,hover,pressed}.png` (game `buttoncollapse*`) | 3 | `[4,5,58,69]` = 54x64 |

## Spacing Scale

Unit is the point. Existing skin tokens are reused (`ROW_PAD`, `ROW_HEIGHT`, `FIELD_GAP = 10`, field height 30, toolbar row 34). This phase adds the following values.

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| xs | 4pt | Header: left inset to the toggle plate; plate-to-bar gap; right-cluster item spacing (unchanged) |
| sm | 8pt | Search field: right inset of the clear X; gap between glyph and text start is 6 (exception) |
| md | 16pt | Magnifier glyph box (16x16) |
| row | 36pt | Section header height (`SECTION_HEADER_HEIGHT`, unchanged) |

Exceptions (all intentional, derived from art proportions or the existing field geometry):
- Toggle plate visible size 27x32pt (art aspect 54:64 at 0.5 pt per art px), 2pt top and bottom inset in the 36pt row.
- Bar scale 36/75 = 0.48 pt per art px (the 75px visible bar height fills the 36pt row): left cap 63pt, right cap 72pt wide.
- Clear X visible disc 18pt (37 art px x 0.486), hit target 24x24pt.
- Magnifier-to-text gap 6pt; left text shift 32pt (10 inset + 16 glyph + 6 gap).
- Everything is pixel-snapped through `slice::snap_rect` at the current `pixels_per_point` (100-200% DPI must stay crisp, no half-pixel seams between bar slices).

## Color

Palette is `src/skin/palette.rs`; no new constants. The art carries its own colours; tints are the only colour logic.

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | `pal::GROUND` / `pal::PANEL` and the stone bar art | window and row surfaces |
| Secondary (30%) | `pal::INPUT_FILL` (field fill), `pal::ROW_HOVER` (bar hover wash) | search field, hovered header |
| Accent (10%) | `pal::GOLD`, `pal::HEADING`, `pal::INPUT_BORDER_HOT` | see list below |
| Destructive | `pal::ERROR` | not used in this phase |

Accent reserved for: header title on hover (`pal::HEADING`), the focus ring of a keyboard-focused header bar and of a focused search field (`pal::INPUT_BORDER_HOT`), the toggle plate's own hover art. The art itself (bronze frame, cream disc) is not "accent".

Tints: art painted with `Color32::WHITE` (no tint) when enabled; disabled reload button keeps `Color32::from_white_alpha(90)`; glyph rest tint for the reload glyph unchanged (`GLYPH_REST_TINT`).

## Component contracts

### 1. Section header (ART-01) — `draw_section_header` in `src/overlay/rule_list.rs`

Row: full list width x 36pt (unchanged, so virtualised heights and Phase 49 slot maths do not move). Left to right inside `rect`:

1. **Toggle plate** visible rect: min = (`rect.min.x + 4`, `rect.min.y + 2`), size 27x32pt. Art is the already-trimmed PNG, painted into exactly that rect (snapped).
   - Not collapsed (displayed state) -> expanded plate (chevron up above dot). Collapsed -> collapsed plate (dot above chevron down). "Displayed state" is the `collapsed` argument, which already ignores the saved fold while searching.
   - States: normal; **hover** art while the pointer is over the plate or the plate/bar has keyboard focus; **pressed** art while the primary button is held anywhere on the bar (the whole bar is one click target and toggles on click) and the pointer is still on the row.
   - The plate is not a separate button: no second `Sense::click` target except a plate-rect interact kept only so the existing accessible "chevron" widget info and tests keep working; its click is the same toggle (as the old chevron).
2. **Stone bar**: starts 4pt right of the plate (x = `rect.min.x + 35`) and ends at `rect.max.x`; height 36pt. Three-slice: left cap 63pt, right cap 72pt (art scale 0.48), middle stretched (UV stretch, not tiled) between them. If the bar is narrower than 135pt the two caps shrink proportionally (never overlap); real windows are 660pt so this is a guard only.
   - Hover: `pal::ROW_HOVER` wash over the bar (clip to the bar) when the pointer is over the row; no art swap (the game has none).
   - Focus (keyboard): 1pt `pal::INPUT_BORDER_HOT` stroke inside the bar rect (existing behaviour, moved onto the bar).
   - The old flat `pal::PANEL` fill and 1pt `INPUT_BORDER` top/bottom lines are removed (the art carries the frame).
3. **Title** (name, or inline rename field) left-aligned at `bar.min.x + 60` so it clears the left cap's swirls (cap is 63pt wide; text starts 3pt inside it, the swirls are at its far end of the art but the text is vertically centred on the darker inset). Left end of text = `inner.min.x` of the old layout replaced by this value; the `{n} rules` count and ellipsis rules are unchanged and take what the right cluster leaves.
4. **Right cluster** (unchanged order and tooltips, right-to-left from `bar.max.x - 12`): remove x, down arrow, up arrow, rename pencil (user sections); padlock (economy). They sit over the right cap art; `item_spacing.x = 4` unchanged.

Economy header: identical plate, bar, hover and focus; padlock stays at the right; the plate toggles the Economy collapsed flag exactly as today (default collapsed -> collapsed plate). Muted/read-only rows below are untouched.

Out of scope: rule rows (Phase 49 swaps their arrows for drag handles), the 3px game gap between bars (the list is contiguous; adding it would change row pitch), the Config style tables.

### 2. Search fields (ART-02, ART-03)

New skin widget `ff_search_field(ui, id, text, hint, width) -> Response` in `src/skin/widgets.rs`, built on the existing `ff_text_field_ex` (same 30pt box, `INPUT_FILL`, 1pt `INPUT_BORDER` / `INPUT_BORDER_HOT` on hover or focus, `FIELD_GAP` behaviour). It takes an explicit id so the clear button can refocus it. Other text fields are unchanged.

Layout inside the 30pt field:
- **Magnifier** (leading): 16x16pt box, left edge 10pt in, vertically centred (x = `field.min.x + 10 .. +26`). `search_hover` art while the field is hovered or focused, `search_normal` (muted) at rest. Decorative, but its zone senses a click and focuses the edit (the zone lies outside the TextEdit rect).
- **Text and placeholder** start at `field.min.x + 32` (was +10); the edit's right edge is `field.max.x - 32` always (space for the X is reserved even when it is hidden, so text does not reflow when the X appears).
- **Clear X** (trailing): visible only while `!text.is_empty()` (not trimmed: a query of spaces still shows it). Disc 18x18pt, right edge 8pt in, vertically centred (centre = `field.max.x - 17`). Hit target 24x24pt centred on the disc (via `ui.interact`, drawn after the edit so it wins clicks). States: normal / hover (hover art, pointing-hand cursor) / pressed (pressed art). Tooltip and accessible name: `Clear search`.
  - Click: empties the text and **keeps focus** in the field (`ctx.memory_mut(|m| m.request_focus(id))`), same effect as Escape in the RULES toolbar. No other state changes (the RULES list reacts to the empty query as it does for Escape; the picker re-shows all sections collapsed).
  - Escape behaviour in the RULES toolbar stays. In the picker popup Escape still closes the popup (unchanged; the X is the way to clear without closing).
- Disabled state: not needed (no search field is ever disabled).

The painted `Clear` text link in `search_row_rects` (`rule_list.rs` ~1477-1498) is deleted and replaced by this widget; the toolbar geometry (field width, `+ Add rule`, `+ Add section`, `FIELD_GAP`) is unchanged.

**Search fields in scope** (every one found in `src/overlay`):

| # | Field | Where | Placeholder (unchanged) |
|---|-------|-------|-------------------------|
| 1 | RULES toolbar search | `rule_list.rs` `search_row_rects` | `Search {n} rules, classes, base types…` |
| 2 | `+ condition` picker search | `body.rs` `picker_popup` (`searchable == true`; its only caller is the `+ condition` button, `body.rs` ~487) | first picker entry hint (existing `hint_text`) |

Searched and found **not** to be search fields (stay plain `ff_text_field*`, no glyph, no X): rule Name, section name (inline rename), style name, filter-draft name, tier cutoffs, colour Hex / R / G / B, the quoted-list `+ Add` entry fields, raw-row editors, the Config filter-name `TextEdit`. The dropdown lists (class picker, sound list, filter list) have no search box.

### 3. Art margins (ART-04)

Rule: **position art by its visible (opaque) plate, never by the texture rect.** Implementation contract:

- New art is trimmed at extraction (table above), so it can be painted straight into its target rect.
- Legacy art keeps its committed PNG (no re-extract churn) and gets a small helper in `src/skin/slice.rs`, e.g. `paint_visible(painter, tex, opaque_px: Rect, dest: Rect, tint)`, which sets the UV to the opaque box and fills `dest` with it (snapped).
- Legacy pieces to fix or confirm: `icon_button_*` (92x92, opaque `[0,0,84,84]`), `reload.png` (glyph, offsets already handled by `RING_CENTRE`), `check_on` / `check_off` (54x56, opaque `[0,0,54,54]`, 2px bottom gutter: use the helper). `close_normal` and `combo_arrow` have no offsetting gutter (no change).

**`ff_reload_button`** (fixes the Config -> Filter refresh offset and the Economy league refresh, which use the same widget; no per-call-site nudging):
- The rect stays `ROW_HEIGHT` x `ROW_HEIGHT` and the **visible plate fills it exactly** (UV cropped to `[0,0,84,84]` of 92). The plate therefore matches the neighbouring dropdown's height and is centred on its row; before, the visible plate was 84/92 of the rect and sat up-left.
- `plate` in the glyph maths becomes `ROW_HEIGHT` (was `ROW_HEIGHT * PLATE_VISIBLE`) and `PLATE_VISIBLE` is removed; ring size stays `0.78` of the plate, centre 1pt below plate centre, pressed 1pt lower, mirror and `-28` degree turn unchanged. Net visible change: the button grows about 9% to full row height and moves down-right by about 1.2pt so it is centred; the glyph scales with it.
- States unchanged: normal / hover-or-focus / pressed art, disabled = dimmed rest art with no hover.
- Call sites need no change (`config_view.rs` ~444 Filter refresh, `shell.rs` ~708 Economy league refresh); verify both with `--screenshot` (`--tab config`, `--tab economy --leagues ...`) that the plate's vertical centre equals the dropdown's.

## Judgement calls (autonomous run; flag for the author's review)

1. **Plate size 27x32pt, not a square 30pt**: the game art is 54x64 art px; stretching it to a square would distort the frame. Kept the art's aspect, 32pt tall so it fits the 36pt row with 2pt margins.
2. **Plate-to-bar gap 4pt**: the reference shows a small gap with the plate "separate from the bar"; 4pt is the smallest skin spacing token.
3. **Bar caps at natural scale (63 / 72pt) and title at +60pt**: keeps the ornate caps undistorted and the title clear of the left swirls, at the cost of ~60pt of title room; the 660pt window still leaves ample width. The right controls sit over the right cap's swirls (game art is mirrored, mild overlap accepted).
4. **No 3pt gap between bars**: the game has one, but the list rows are contiguous and Phase 49 builds on current row pitch; bars fill the full 36pt.
5. **Reload button grows to fill the row rather than centring a smaller plate**: matches the dropdown beside it; the alternative (keep 84/92 size, centre it) leaves a visibly shorter button next to the dropdown.
6. **No italic placeholder**: Fontin Regular has no italic face and no new fonts are allowed; the game's italic grey is approximated by `pal::DISABLED`.
7. **X is not a tab stop and is shown for whitespace-only text**: avoids changing keyboard order; a query of spaces is still a non-empty field.
8. **Space for the X is always reserved** so the typed text never reflows when the X appears.
9. **Magnifier `pressed` art not extracted**: the glyph is decorative; hover art is used for hover/focus of the whole field.
10. **Only two search fields exist** (list above); the quoted-list `+ Add` fields are entry fields, not search, so they get no glyph or X.
11. **Legacy PNGs are not re-extracted** (no binary churn, `ff-art` stays idempotent for them): a UV-crop helper handles the margin instead; the new art is trimmed at extraction.
