<objective>
Make both features usable: a name field at the top of the expanded body (D-16), the class picker
demoted to an optional condition that disappears when switched off (D-06/D-07/D-08), and a
base-type list built one entry at a time with per-item removal (D-01/D-02/D-03/D-14) sitting
directly after the class control (D-16).

The one genuinely new mechanism here is RESEARCH Pattern 1. Every existing condition toggle derives
its checked state from `when.<field>.is_some()` every frame and seeds a non-empty default the
instant it is switched on. `class`, `name` and `base_type` cannot do that: D-09 forbids
auto-selecting a class, D-11 forbids auto-filling a name, and there is no sensible default base
type. A derived toggle would therefore read back `false` on the very next repaint and the panel
would snap shut before the player could click or type anything — not a polish problem, a
non-functional control. These three toggles back their state with egui's own id-keyed ephemeral
store instead, written once by the click and left alone by the render loop.

The entry mechanics beyond "item by item, removable anywhere" were left to Claude's discretion
(D-04) and are already locked as D-14 (quote stripping and paste splitting), D-15 (the `Bases:`
summary label, delivered in plan 15-01) and D-16 (body order) — this plan implements those, it does
not reopen them.

Everything else here is composition the file already does: `circular_toggle` + value, `id_salt`ed
by `RuleId`, deferred collect-then-apply for a mutation discovered mid-loop, and every mutation
routed through `edit::`.

Purpose: roadmap criteria 1, 2 and 4 become reachable from the stripe rather than only from data;
RESEARCH Pitfalls 1, 4 and 5 are closed here.

Output: `src/overlay/rule_editor.rs`, extended — no other file changes, because
`render_rule_body`'s signature and its single call site in `app.rs` are already exactly what the
new controls need.
</objective>
