  `load_priced(paths, policy)`; `load_priced` (~L256) reads `paths.tiers` in the Live and the
  no-prices-anywhere arms and `tiers_fallback` in the fallback arms. `compile_economy(&EconomyInputs)`

  `apply_edited(loaded, edited_text, op)` (~L349): FilterNotFound when absent+empty+not Add, parse,

  `NoEconomySection`/`Section`, then `write_new_text` (~L273) which runs

  `LoadedFilter::rebase_to_disk` (~L190).
