<objective>
Turn the raw-line document into a structured, read-only index: blocks (live and disabled) with
stable IDs, attached leading comments, `#ff:` metadata, Import directives, opaque/unknown lines, and
line-tied diagnostics — without ever changing a stored line.

structural half of PARSE-02 (Show/Hide/Minimal/Continue/Import recognized as grammar), while
PARSE-03 byte identity continues to hold. Also defines the D-05..D-09 cross-cutting concepts that
Phases 18-20 depend on.
Output: `src/filter/{block,parse,disabled,meta}.rs`, extended `Document`, corpus integration tests.
</objective>
